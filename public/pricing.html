Pricing

LuggageShare offers a flexible pricing model, allowing carriers to set their own price per kilogram for the space they offer. As a sender, you will pay the price set by the carrier for the space you need.

How Pricing Works:

  • Carriers Set Price: When posting an ad, carriers specify the price they charge per kilogram for their available space.
  • Senders Agree to Price: Senders view the price per kg when browsing ads and agree to this rate when requesting space.
  • Total Cost Calculation: The total cost for a sender is calculated based on the agreed-upon price per kg multiplied by the weight of the luggage.
  • Platform Fee: LuggageShare may charge a small service fee on successful transactions to cover operational costs and maintain the platform. Details about any applicable fees will be clearly communicated during the agreement process.

Payment:

Payments are processed securely through the platform after the successful completion of the luggage transfer, as confirmed by both parties.

Our goal is to provide a transparent and fair pricing system that benefits both carriers and senders.
