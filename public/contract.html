Luggage Space Sharing Agreement

This agreement is made between the Space Provider and the Luggage Owner

1. Definitions

"Space Provider" refers to the individual offering their luggage space.

"Luggage Owner" refers to the individual whose luggage will be transported.

"Luggage" refers to the items being transported in the Space Provider's luggage space.

2. Agreement Terms

2.1 The Space Provider agrees to transport the Luggage Owner's items in their available luggage space.

2.2 The Luggage Owner agrees to pay the agreed-upon fee for the luggage space.

2.3 Both parties agree to comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding luggage transportation.

3. Liability and Responsibility

3.1 The Luggage Owner acknowledges and agrees that they are solely responsible for:

  • The legality of all items being transported
  • Ensuring all items comply with airline and customs regulations
  • Any customs duties, taxes, or fees that may be incurred
  • Any legal consequences arising from the contents of their luggage

3.2 The Space Provider shall not be held liable for:

  • Any illegal items found in the luggage
  • Customs seizures or delays
  • Any legal consequences arising from the contents of the luggage

4. Luggage Contents Declaration

4.1 The Luggage Owner must declare all contents of their luggage to the Space Provider.

4.2 The Luggage Owner must ensure all items are legal and comply with airline regulations.

4.3 The Space Provider reserves the right to refuse transportation of any items they deem suspicious or potentially illegal.

5. Payment Terms

5.1 The agreed payment amount must be made through the platform's secure payment system.

5.2 Payment will be released to the Space Provider upon successful delivery and confirmation.

6. Cancellation Policy

6.1 Either party may cancel the agreement up to 24 hours before the scheduled flight.

6.2 Cancellation fees may apply as per the platform's standard policy.
