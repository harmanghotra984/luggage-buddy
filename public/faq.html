Frequently Asked Questions
  • What is Luggage Buddy?
    Luggage Buddy is a platform that connects air travelers with excess baggage capacity to those who need extra luggage space, helping both parties save money and travel more efficiently.
  • How do I post or find luggage space?
    After signing up and logging in, you can post an ad for available luggage space or browse ads to find someone offering space for your needs.
  • Is it safe to use Luggage Buddy?
    Yes, all users are authenticated, and agreements are formalized with a contract module to ensure accountability and transparency.
  • How are payments and agreements handled?
    All terms are negotiated in a private chat, and a legally binding agreement is generated for both parties to review and accept.
  • Who is responsible if something goes wrong?
    The contract clearly states that the luggage owner is responsible for the contents. If illegal items are found, the owner bears full responsibility.
