Safety

Your safety is our top priority at LuggageShare. We implement several measures to ensure a secure and trustworthy platform:

User Verification:

All users are encouraged to complete our verification process, which may include confirming email addresses and phone numbers. This helps build a more reliable community.

Secure Communication:

Our platform offers a private chat system, allowing users to communicate without sharing personal contact information until they are comfortable.

Formal Agreements:

We provide a feature to create formal agreements (contracts) outlining the terms of the luggage transfer, including details about the items, compensation, and responsibilities. This adds a layer of security and clarity to each transaction.

Reporting System:

If you encounter any suspicious activity or issues with another user, you can report it to our support team. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

Safety Guidelines:

We strongly recommend users follow our safety guidelines, such as meeting in public places, verifying the contents of the luggage (within legal and privacy boundaries), and keeping records of all communications and agreements.

While we take measures to promote safety, please remember to exercise caution and good judgment when interacting with others on the platform.
