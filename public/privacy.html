Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

Luggage Buddy is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and never share your personal data with third parties except as required by law or to fulfill our service obligations. All sensitive data is securely stored and encrypted.

Your data is used solely to facilitate connections between travelers, manage ads, and ensure the security of all transactions. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
