How It Works

LuggageShare connects individuals who have extra space in their luggage with those who need to send items. Here's a simple breakdown:

For those with extra space (Carriers):

  • Post an Ad: Create a listing detailing your flight route, date, available luggage weight/space, and price per kg.
  • Receive Requests: Interested users looking to send items will contact you through the platform's chat.
  • Agree on Terms: Discuss details like item specifics, meeting points, and finalize the agreement using our built-in contract feature.
  • Transport Luggage: Carry the agreed-upon luggage on your flight.
  • Get Paid: Receive payment securely through the platform upon successful delivery.

For those who need space (Senders):

  • Search Ads: Browse available listings based on your destination and travel date.
  • Contact Carrier: Reach out to a suitable carrier through the private chat to discuss your needs.
  • Agree on Terms: Finalize details and create a formal agreement with the carrier.
  • Handover Luggage: Meet the carrier at the agreed location (e.g., airport) to hand over your luggage.
  • Track and Receive: Track your luggage's journey and receive it at the destination.

LuggageShare provides a secure platform for communication, agreement, and payment to ensure a smooth experience for both parties.
